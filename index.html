🧟 ZOMBIE WAVES VR

Defend your space from goofy, blocky zombies! Survive each wave, earn gems, and upgrade your colorful energy blasters.

How to play

🖐️ VR: Blasters appear in your controller hands automatically. Aim at a zombie and press the trigger to shoot. No grip button needed.

🖱️ Desktop: Click to shoot; use WASD to move. You can test the game without a headset.

🧟 Each zombie starts with 100 HP. Defeating one gives you 30 gems. Clear a wave to unlock the next one.

Passthrough requires a compatible headset/browser and permission. If passthrough is unavailable, the game uses a normal 3D background.
